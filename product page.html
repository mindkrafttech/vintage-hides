Got Questions?

What fits inside the Ryza Core bag? +
The Ryza Core fits your daily essentials including an iPhone 15 Pro Max, up to 4 cards, house keys, a lipstick, and a 5ml perfume bottle. It also has an inner slip pocket for quick access to cards or cash. Dimensions: 20 × 13 × 5 cm. Weight: 320 grams.
Is the Ryza Core made from real leather or synthetic material? +
Yes — 100% genuine full-grain LWG-Gold certified leather. Not PU, not bonded, not synthetic. Full-grain is the highest quality grade and develops a natural patina that makes your bag more characterful over time. The same grade used in luxury bags costing ₹20,000 and above.
Is Cash on Delivery available for the Ryza Core in India? +
Yes. Cash on Delivery is available across most pin codes in India including Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities. A small ₹99 courier handling fee applies for COD. Prepaid orders via UPI, card, or net banking get an additional 10% OFF plus free shipping.
How long does delivery take for the Ryza Core across India? +
Prepaid orders are dispatched within 24 hours and delivered in 3–5 working days across India via BlueDart. Enter your pincode on this page to see the exact estimated delivery date for your location.
What is the return policy for the Ryza Core? +
7-day easy returns. If you are not satisfied for any reason, contact support within 7 days of delivery. A free pickup will be arranged from your doorstep and the full refund is processed within 5–7 business days. The bag also comes with a lifetime hardware warranty.
How do I care for the Ryza Core genuine leather bag? +
Wipe gently with a soft dry cloth after each use. Avoid heavy rain, extreme sunlight, and prolonged moisture. Store in the included branded dust bag when not in use. Apply a mild leather conditioner every 6 months. Full-grain leather gets more beautiful and personal with time.
What colours is the Ryza Core available in? +
The Ryza Core is available in four colours: Pitch Black, Mocha Brown, Cream Beige, and Olive Green. All colours use the same 100% genuine full-grain LWG-certified leather. Select your colour on this page and the product image updates to show the exact shade.
Is the Ryza Core genuinely handcrafted in India? +
Yes. Every Ryza Core bag is handcrafted in India by skilled leather artisans. Because it uses genuine full-grain leather, minor natural variations in texture, grain, and colour are expected — they are a mark of authenticity, not a defect. No two Ryza Core bags are exactly the same.
