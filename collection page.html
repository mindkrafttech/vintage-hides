Got Questions?

Everything about the Ryza Series.

What is the Ryza Series by Vintage Hides? +
The Ryza Series is a collection of 7 genuine full-grain LWG-certified leather handbags for women by Vintage Hides. Each design is built for a specific use — from minimal everyday carry (Ryza Core, Ryza Duo) to bold statement styling (Ryza Croco, Ryza Muse). All bags are handcrafted in Vaniyambadi, Tamil Nadu, and priced under ₹6,000.
Which Ryza bag is best for everyday use? +
The Ryza Halo is best for everyday use — structured, spacious, and works for office, commuting, and casual outings. For minimal everyday carry, the Ryza Core (₹3,499) and Ryza Duo (₹3,999) are ideal. For a bold daily statement, the Ryza Croco or Ryza Muse are top picks.
Are Vintage Hides Ryza bags made from real leather? +
Yes. Every Ryza bag is made from 100% genuine full-grain LWG-Gold certified leather. Full-grain is the highest quality leather grade — it does not peel or crack like synthetic PU leather, and develops a natural patina over time. Leather is sourced from Vaniyambadi, Tamil Nadu — one of India's most established leather manufacturing hubs.
What is the price range of the Ryza Series? +
The Ryza Series ranges from ₹3,499 (Ryza Core) to ₹5,999 (Ryza Croco, Ryza Muse editions). Ryza Halo ₹4,499 · Ryza Muse ₹5,499 · Ryza Duo ₹3,999. All bags include free shipping on prepaid orders. COD available with a ₹99 handling fee. Use code RYZA10 for 10% off prepaid.
Is Cash on Delivery available for Ryza Series bags? +
Yes. COD is available for all Ryza Series bags across most Indian pin codes including Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities. A ₹99 handling fee applies for COD. Prepaid orders via UPI or card receive 10% off (RYZA10) and free express shipping. Delivery takes 3–5 working days via BlueDart.
Where are Ryza bags made in India? +
All Ryza bags are handcrafted in India using leather from Vaniyambadi, Tamil Nadu — a town with decades of leather artisan heritage. Vaniyambadi produces leather used by global brands. Vintage Hides uses this local supply chain to offer premium LWG-certified leather at accessible prices under ₹6,000.
What is the return policy for the Ryza Series? +
Vintage Hides offers 72-hour easy returns on all Ryza bags, with a 1-year repair warranty on hardware and stitching. Contact support via WhatsApp within 72 hours of delivery. Free pickup is arranged from your doorstep and the full refund is processed within 5–7 business days.
How do I choose between the different Ryza bags? +
Choose by use case: Ryza Core for minimal carry (phone, cards, keys). Ryza Duo for compact everyday with two compartments. Ryza Halo for structured daily use. Ryza Muse for statement styling. Ryza Croco for bold textured fashion. Muse Black and Brown Editions for classic premium looks. All use the same full-grain LWG leather — the choice is purely size and aesthetic.
