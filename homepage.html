Got Questions?

Everything you might want to know.

What fits inside the Ryza Muse? +
The Ryza Muse fits your daily essentials — cards, keys, earrings, a 5ml perfume, and makeup — with a backside slip pocket for quick access. Dimensions: 22 × 14 × 6 cm. Weight: 350g. It comfortably holds an iPhone 15 Pro Max alongside daily carry items.
Is this real leather or synthetic? +
Genuine full-grain LWG-Gold certified leather — not PU, not bonded, not synthetic. Full-grain is the highest quality grade and develops a natural patina over time. It is the same grade used in luxury bags costing ₹20,000 and above at Indian boutiques.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery across India? +
Yes. Cash on Delivery is available across most pin codes in India including Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities. A small ₹99 convenience fee applies for COD. Prepaid and partial pay options are also available with 5–10% additional discounts and free express shipping.
What if I receive a damaged product? +
If there is any damage, contact us within 7 days of delivery. We offer a full replacement or refund with no questions asked. COD orders also come with Open Box Delivery, allowing you to inspect the bag at the doorstep before paying.
How long does delivery take across India? +
We deliver across India in 3–5 working days via BlueDart. Prepaid orders are dispatched within 24 hours. Enter your pincode on the product page to see the exact estimated delivery date for your location.
What is the return policy? +
7-day easy returns. If you are not satisfied, contact support within 7 days of delivery. A free pickup will be arranged from your doorstep and the full refund is processed within 5–7 business days. All bags also come with a 1-year hardware warranty on stitching and clasps.
How do I care for a genuine leather bag? +
Wipe gently with a soft dry cloth after each use. Avoid heavy rain and prolonged direct sunlight. Store in the included branded dust bag when not in use. Apply a mild leather conditioner every 6 months. Full-grain leather develops a beautiful natural patina — it gets better the more you use it.
What is the price of Vintage Hides leather bags? +
The Ryza Collection starts at ₹3,499 (Ryza Core) and goes up to ₹5,999 (Ryza Croco). Ryza Muse ₹5,499 · Ryza Halo ₹4,499 · Ryza Duo ₹3,999. All bags use the same 100% full-grain LWG-certified leather. Prepaid orders get an additional 10% off using code RYZA10.
