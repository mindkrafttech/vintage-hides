Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about becoming a Vintage Hides partner.

How does the Vintage Hides affiliate programme work? +
Apply for free and get a unique referral link and custom discount code. Share it on WhatsApp, Instagram, or anywhere. When someone buys through your link, you earn 10–15% commission. Weekly payouts directly to your UPI. No investment, no stock, no delivery — Vintage Hides handles everything.
How much can I actually earn as a partner? +
Each sale earns Rs.400–Rs.700+ depending on the bag. Selling 10–15 bags per month generates Rs.5,000–Rs.10,000. Top partners promoting consistently on WhatsApp and Instagram earn Rs.15,000–Rs.30,000 per month. Commission rates are 10–15% with bonus incentives for high performers.
Is there any investment or joining fee? +
Zero investment and zero joining fee. 100% free to join. You do not need to buy stock, manage delivery, or handle returns. Vintage Hides handles all logistics. You only share your link and earn when sales happen.
How and when do I get paid? +
Weekly via UPI directly to your bank account. Minimum payout threshold is Rs.500. All payouts are TDS-compliant and documented. Track your earnings, clicks, and conversion rates in real time on the partner dashboard.
Does Vintage Hides offer Cash on Delivery for my audience? +
Yes. All bags are available on Cash on Delivery across most Indian pin codes when ordered through your link. Open Box Delivery is also available — customers inspect the bag at the doorstep before paying. This removes all hesitation and significantly increases your conversion rate.
What do I need to apply? +
An active WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube, or social presence. No minimum follower count required. A UPI-linked bank account for payouts. A valid PAN card for TDS compliance. Application takes 2 minutes and approval is typically within 24 hours.
What marketing materials do you provide? +
The Content Kit includes product images, pre-written WhatsApp messages, Instagram caption templates, reel hook scripts, objection-handling guides, and product PDFs. All designed for the Indian social media audience. Ready to post immediately — no content creation needed to start.
Is this available across all of India? +
Yes. Promote to customers anywhere in India — Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 cities. Delivery via BlueDart across most pin codes. COD available at most locations. Your local WhatsApp contacts and regional audiences convert just as well as metro customers.
